Sticker share test

This page checks one thing: when a web page shares a transparent WebP file to WhatsApp, does it arrive as a sticker or as a photo?

What to look for in WhatsApp

  1. Sticker: no chat bubble, no caption box before sending, and the checkerboard area is see-through. You can tap it and pick "Add to Favorites".
  2. Photo: it opens the photo editor with a caption box, and arrives inside a bubble, often with a black or white background.

Send each test to your own "Message yourself" chat.
